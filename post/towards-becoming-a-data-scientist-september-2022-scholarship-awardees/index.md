Dear Applicant,

We are happy to inform you that you have been awarded a Data Insight Scholarship for the [Data Scientist Program](https://shoutout.wix.com/so/45NjblfU_/c?w=h600hUEpni7wmTuK39Re4-zmsVUiNAH6eB4MHBc7UCU.eyJ1IjoiaHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuZGF0YWluc2lnaHRvbmxpbmUuY29tL2RhdGEtc2NpZW50aXN0LXByb2dyYW0iLCJyIjoiNzFjYzA2MWEtMzViOC00NTM4LTgzYTctZTc4OTgyNTVkZDVhIiwibSI6ImxwIn0). Please accept our hearty **congratulations!**

[***Check the list of successful applicants.***](https://www.datainsightonline.com/sep2022-scholarship-awardees)

As you begin your journey towards becoming a data expert this year, we are excited to begin this exciting and life-changing journey with you. The Data Scientist Program will require a lot of self-motivation and dedication. You will sometimes go through difficult challenges, however, at the end of it all, it will be worthwhile and exceedingly rewarding.

The [Data Scientist Program](https://www.datainsightonline.com/data-scientist-program) will begin on **September 1, 2022**, and end on **August 31, 2023**. Throughout this period of one year, you will take courses and complete practical projects while building your data science portfolio. You will be assigned course work and projects, which you must complete, at your own convenience, within the stipulated deadline.

You have been invited to the **Data Insight Data Scientist Program Group** via the email you provided with your application.

Communicating your ideas is very important in data science. In this program, you will be doing a lot of writing in order to communicate your work. So you are required to create an account on [Data Insight](https://www.datainsightonline.com/blog), [GitHub](https://github.com/), and [Medium](https://medium.com/).

For day-to-day communication with regard to the [Data Scientist Program](https://www.datainsightonline.com/data-scientist-program), you have been invited to join a dedicated [**Slack Channel**](https://join.slack.com/t/data-insight-online/shared_invite/https://join.slack.com/t/data-insight-online/shared_invite/zt-uuocga2p-Aem3o40kAOjrl2rqX7yHHg). In addition to this, you should **follow the** [**Data Insight page on LinkedIn**](https://www.linkedin.com/company/data-insight-academy).

We wish you the very best in this program.

[Data Insight Team](https://www.datainsightonline.com/)
