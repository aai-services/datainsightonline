As you embark on your journey toward becoming a data science professional, we look forward to supporting you through this transformative experience. This program is designed to challenge and empower you, equipping you with the tools, knowledge, and practical experience to thrive in the dynamic world of data.

### Program Overview

- **Start Date:** July 1, 2025
- **End Date:** June 30, 2026

Over a period of one year, you will:

- Complete carefully curated courses
- Tackle real-world projects
- Build a comprehensive data science portfolio

Assignments and projects will be self-paced but must be completed within specified deadlines. This structure is designed to give you flexibility while fostering accountability and time management, crucial skills for any data scientist.

### Getting Started

To help you stay connected and make the most of this fellowship, please take the following steps:

- **Join the Data Insight Group:** An invitation has been sent to the email address you provided during the application process.
- **Set Up Your Accounts:** You are required to create accounts on:

  - [Data Insight](https://www.datainsightonline.com/blog)
  - [GitHub](https://github.com/)
  - [Medium](https://medium.com/)

    These platforms will be essential for showcasing your projects and writing about your insights.
- **Stay Connected:**

  - Join the dedicated **Slack channel** (invitation sent via email) for day-to-day updates and community support.
  - Follow [Data Insight on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/data-insight-academy) for announcements and networking opportunities.

### What to Expect

This program demands self-motivation, consistency, and resilience. There will be challenges along the way, but the knowledge and confidence you gain will be immensely rewarding. By the end of this journey, you will have developed strong analytical skills, technical expertise, and a compelling professional portfolio.

We are honored to welcome you into the 2025 cohort of Data Insight Fellows. This opportunity marks the beginning of an exciting chapter, and we can’t wait to see the impact you’ll make.
