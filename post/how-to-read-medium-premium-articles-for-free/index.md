The [Medium](https://medium.com) blogging/publishing platform allows you to read a couple of premium articles for free every month. Otherwise you have to pay a recurrent fee each month to read premium articles.

In this short life hack you will learn how to read unlimited number of premium articles on [Medium](https://medium.com) for free by browsing in private mode or incognito. To do this, simply

1. Copy the URL of the premium article.
2. Press Control+Shift+N. This will take you incognito.
3. Paste and search the copied URL.

**You are in!**

## Notes:

- Instead of pressing Control+Shift+N, you can also open a private browsing session on your browser.
- For MAC users, use Command button instead of Control.
- For the meaning of incognito see [dictionary.cambridge.org](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/incognito).
- "Private browsing is a [privacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_privacy) feature in some [web browsers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Web_browser). When operating in such a mode, the browser creates a temporary [session](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Session_(computer_science)) that is [isolated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interface_segregation_principle) from the browser's main session and user data. [Browsing history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Web_browsing_history) is not saved, and local data associated with the session, such as [cookies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HTTP_cookie), are cleared when the session is closed. These modes are designed primarily to prevent data and history associated with a particular browsing session from persisting on the device, or being discovered by another user of the same device. ~Wikipedia
